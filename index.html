VIEW GALLERY COLLECTION — 26 WORKS Original Works ABOUT Diana Shirinova FOLK ART FIGURATIVE ACRYLIC HAWAIIAN PRIMITIVISM ABSTRACT These paintings explore the feminine spirit of the Hawaiian Islands through a folk art lens — simplified forms, flat color, and symbolic imagery drawn from island culture: hibiscus, monstera, waves, surfboards, and the timeless silhouette of the Hawaiian woman. Each piece is hand-painted on canvas in Honolulu. The style draws from modern primitivism — bold outlines, expressive brushwork, and a palette pulled from the Hawaiian landscape: terracotta sunsets, sage greens, ocean blues, and warm earth tones.
