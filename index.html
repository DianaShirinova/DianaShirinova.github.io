The Process

It begins with colour

Diana rarely paints with colour straight from the tube. Each work begins at the palette, where pigments are mixed by hand until the right harmony appears for that painting.

This is where the character of a piece is set. Small shifts between one tone and the next carry the light and atmosphere of the islands into the finished work.

About

Diana Shirinova

Acrylic Hawaiian Light Hand-Mixed Original Works Fine Art Prints

Diana Shirinova is a contemporary painter based in Honolulu. Her work draws on the light of the Hawaiian Islands — the ocean, tropical flowers, and a quiet feminine presence.

Each painting is built from a palette mixed by hand, which gives every work its own colour and atmosphere.

Original paintings are released at select art markets on Oʻahu and by direct inquiry — many earlier works now reside in private collections. Selected works are also available as Fine Art Prints.
